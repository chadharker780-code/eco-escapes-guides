Section 02

Adding or updating documents

Three rules govern every decision below: find before you create, update over replace, archive instead of delete. Pick the lane that matches what you're trying to do and follow the steps. The same flow applies to a new SOP, a renters agreement update, or an obsolete checklist.

Rule 01
Find before you create. Always search Drive first.
Rule 02
Update over replace. Don't make parallel copies.
Rule 03
Archive, never delete. Old content moves to ARCHIVE.
